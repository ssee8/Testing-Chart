1.b Batteries
SlParameter to be verifiedAction Taken / Actual Value
1
Check for any leakage from battery.
2
Check electrolyte level and top up with distilled water, if necessary.
3
Remove any dirt deposits or electrolyte spillage.
4
Check Individual cell voltage (1.2 - 1.4 V).
5
Check specific gravity of electrolyte (1.190 - 1.210).
6
Clean & check tightness of electrical connections and apply white petroleum jelly.
7
Check condition of vent plugs and secure properly.Ensure flame arrestors are invariably provided in all vent caps (RDSO/PE/SMI/TL/0046-2012).
8
Check the healthiness and tightness of control wires.
9
Boost charging to be done with 150 V; 4–5 hours; max 40 A (IC, Ref: RDSO PE SMI 0046)
2. Battery Box
SlParameter to be verifiedAction Taken / Actual Value
1
Visually inspect battery box for external hits & damages. Repair, If required.
2
Visually check the hanging supports of battery box.
3
Ensure DPT to check for any cracks on the hanging supports.
4
Ensure proper provisioning of inter battery packing spacers.
5
Clean the battery, battery box and tray.
6
Check tightness of fixing bolts between battery box and support.
7
Check door seal, door lock. Test locking handles. Repair, If required.
8
Apply small amount of suitable lubricant to locking handle pivots.
9
Apply grease to sliding mechanism and check freeness of operation.
10
Check cable layout and its rubbing inside battery box.
11
Check cable /ventilation hose fitted on back side of battery box.
12
Check breather provided on battery box.
13
Check earthing cables of battery box.
14
Check male/female battery connection.
15
Check Stiffener tightness on battery connection.
3. Battery Box MCB (112)
SlParameter to be verifiedAction Taken / Actual Value
1
Check tightness of battery box MCB.
2
Check tightness of battery box MCB connection.
3
Check for any rubbing of MCB cable.
4
Check earthing cable in MCB box.
5
Check MCB operation and MCB cover fitment.
4. Head Light
SlParameter to be verifiedAction Taken / Actual Value
1
Clean the glasses of the HL unit and ensure no crack.
2
Ensure no water entry through head light joints.
3
Check for crack/breakage of aluminium casing of headlight.
4
Check fitment and tightness of headlight.
5
Check the proper operation of head light.
5. Cab, Machine Room and Spotlight
SlParameter to be verifiedAction Taken / Actual Value
1
Clean all light fittings and ensure its intactness.
2
Check spotlight connections and condition of its holder.
3
Check tube light connection for tightness and cable condition.
4
Check working of latching switch 327.1 and 327.2
5
Check the connection and tightness of terminal bar
6
Check proper operation of lights. Replace if defective.
7
Check the condition of XK32R & K62R coupler
6. Marker Light
SlParameter to be verifiedAction Taken / Actual Value
1
Clean the glasses of the marker and ensure no crack.
2
Ensure no entry of water through marker light and apply sealant.
3
Check proper operation of marker lights (red & white).
7. Signal Exchange Lamps
SlParameter to be verifiedAction Taken / Actual Value
1.
Check terminal connections and water sealing of signal exchange lamps.
2.
Check condition of signal exchange lamp control units and push-buttons.
3.
Check Red & Green Lamps glowing in both cab LP and ALP sides.
8. Cables
SlParameter to be verifiedAction Taken / Actual Value
1
Visually check for rubbing mark or damage of cables and attend.
2
Check for overheating/damage of cables. Attend if noted.
3
Check condition of TMDDS cables and its stiffeners.
11. Machine Room Foot Plates Over Cable Trenches
SlParameter to be verifiedAction Taken / Actual Value
1
Check intactness of all fasteners fixing foot plates in machine room.
12. LTBA
SlParameter to be verifiedAction Taken / Actual Value
1.
Check LTBA for overheating marks. Clean LTBA top glass.
2.
Check LTBA connections for proper tightness.
9. Inter Vehicle Couplers (IV Couplers)
SlParameter to be verifiedAction Taken / Actual Value
1
Check IV coupler cover/body for damage and replace if required.
2
Check visually for damage, flash mark and overheating.
3
Check intactness of sealing ring gasket on socket of locomotive.
4
Clean IV coupler and lubricate moving parts if found sluggish.
5
Check Ratchet assembly locking/unlocking & intactness of all hardware.
6
Ensure intactness of both HEX small bolt and HEX big bolt.
7
Ensure proper working of locking bolt.
8
Ensure intactness of both Allen screw.
9
Ensure intactness of insulated base foundation bolt.
10
Ensure Lever tie bow assembly gap with “NO GO Gauge”.
11
Check IV coupler mobile contact pin (Phase & control) for freeness.
12
Clean coupler pins with suitable cleaners.
13
Ensure couplers are in closed conditions to prevent dust/water.
14
Check phase sequence at all 4 couplers.
15
Replace O ring (sealing gasket) of IV coupler. (IC only)
16
Check Insulation between Control Pins (C1 & C2) w.r.t body (>10 M ohm).
10. UIC Couplers
SlParameter to be verifiedAction Taken / Actual Value
1
Check water leakage through joints, gasket condition & apply sealant.
2
Ensure proper locking of couplers.
3
Ensure UIC couplers are painted green on both sides.
4
Check both sides UIC coupler cover tension. Replace if improper.
5
Check female contact pin for oxidation/flashing, diameter and locking.
6
UIC coupler must be in closed condition preventing dust and water.
7
In UIC coupler, condition of all red color gaskets needs to be checked.
8
LP and ALP UIC couplers all pins continuity checked with tester.
